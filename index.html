About This App

The Karma Landscape app is used to log and track landscaping maintenance tasks (such as watering, fertilizing, spraying, and pruning), scan asset QR codes on plants, and record service updates.

This page details what information the app uses, how it is stored, and how an account can be deleted.

Data We Collect & How We Use It

We only collect the operational information necessary for the application to function:

Data Type Details Purpose
Account Credentials Assigned Username (e.g., custom handle/code) and internal authentication identifier. To sign you in and verify your access role. The app does not collect personal email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords for account creation.
Maintenance Records Selected maintenance action, item identifier, and record timestamp. To log completed plant maintenance and generate service report documents.
Inspection Photos Before and after photos taken within the app. To document work completion and plant condition. Images are securely hosted on Firebase Cloud Storage.
App State Active visit status stored locally on the device. To ensure active progress is not lost if the app closes unexpectedly.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data, and we do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

Permissions Used by the App

  • Camera: Used exclusively to scan asset QR codes and capture still inspection photos. The app does not record audio, does not request microphone access, and does not record video.
  • Storage / Media: Used solely to save generated PDF reports or QR code images to your device, or to save captured photos locally.
  • Internet: Required to authenticate sessions, sync maintenance logs, and upload photos to Firebase.

Note: We do not track, collect, or request GPS location data.

Services We Use

  • Google Firebase: Firebase Authentication manages secure sign-in verification, Cloud Firestore stores maintenance logs, and Cloud Storage hosts inspection images.
  • WhatsApp: When scanning an asset code in the client view, the app can open a pre-filled WhatsApp message on your device to reach customer support. The app does not read your private chats or contact list.

Account & Data Deletion

Users have the right to request the deletion of their username account and associated data at any time.

How to request deletion:

  1. Send an email to: support@karmalandscape.com
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Account
  3. State your assigned Username.
Contact: support@karmalandscape.com
Requests are processed and the account is permanently deleted within 14 days of receipt.

What happens when an account is deleted:

  • The assigned username and internal authentication profile are permanently removed from the system.
  • Historical plant maintenance entries (such as a record that an asset was watered on a specific date) remain in the system for service history, but are completely detached from the deleted username.
